# Reader publications

The writer reads accepted, immutable research reports and the originals already frozen by those reports. It does not redo collection, introduce facts, infer consensus, or turn a missing input into certainty. The checker is a separate `review`-profile run and compares every material number, unit, fiscal period, source link, counterexample and causal statement with the frozen input.

Company reports explain: one-minute judgment; the business and customer; three to five meaningful operating changes; profit, working capital and cash; a possible opportunity through change, independent evidence, profit capture, persistence, expectation gap and validation; the strongest counterevidence; three conditional scenarios; and the next observable check. IPO variants also cover concentration, acquisition/fulfilment cost, cash runway, use of proceeds, primary versus secondary shares, dilution, related parties and lockups.

Industry reports explain the chain, full frozen sample including negative and missing observations, breadth versus share shifts or restocking, profit transmission, incomparable metrics, candidate beneficiaries and losers, counterevidence, coverage and next-quarter checks. Quarterly roles reread decisive originals; the publication may not merely compress daily summaries.

Market reports are titled “美股重点行业季度研究” and state the five-industry/30-company frozen coverage. They independently compare operating change, breadth, cash quality, shared customers and capex-to-revenue timing. Do not count one customer's demand repeatedly across suppliers. Rank only the next research priorities and validation timing, never deterministic trades or an opaque alpha score.

Markdown is authoritative; local HTML is derived. Parse contract numeric strings as Decimal, preserving sign. Every displayed financial quantity, including every table cell and legitimate reuse, must bind by exact occurrence to an evidence id, original metric, currency/unit, fiscal period, accounting basis and supported derivation/rounding. The runner supplies its deterministic occurrence inventory: prose spans are Python string codepoint offsets into the exact Markdown with commas retained, while table coordinates are one-based line/column. The model validates evidence bindings but never counts offsets or computes hashes. Writer instructions require supported explicit units, unit-bearing table headers and machine-checkable duration/instant period wording. The semantic check must be error-free; the runner—not the model—binds draft, input-manifest and source hashes. Only registered live research with a completed task is publishable. A report with missing scope boundaries may use one unique evidence-supported standalone period without mutating the report; ambiguity remains an actionable gap. Expand reference-style web links; remove local anchors and filesystem-relative links from cloud import. Readback equivalence permits only documented representation changes such as a leading `<title>` wrapper and table-separator alignment normalization; cells, text and link targets remain exact. Create/update/fetch uses the explicit configured user route and cwd-relative `@./prepared.md`. A changed remote baseline is a conflict, and an ambiguous create is `unknown` until explicit read-only reconciliation.
