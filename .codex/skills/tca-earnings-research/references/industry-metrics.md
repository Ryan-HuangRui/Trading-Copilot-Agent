# Industry metric templates

These are research questions, not universal scoring formulas or trading rules. Use metrics only when disclosed with comparable definitions. Explain changes in definitions and missing fields.

| Theme | Demand and operating measures | Main counterchecks |
|---|---|---|
| semiconductors | segment revenue, units/ASP, inventory, bookings, utilization, customer capex | channel restocking, double ordering, product mix, customer concentration, capacity cycle |
| cloud-software | segment revenue, consumption, retention, remaining obligations/backlog, customer budgets, cloud capacity spend | prepaid/multiyear distortions, usage vs bookings, stock compensation, cash collection, concentrated demand |
| electrification-industrial | organic growth, orders, backlog conversion, margins, delivery times, capacity investment | cancellable orders, project delays, commodity pass-through, one-off acquisitions, supply normalization |
| managed-care | membership, premiums, medical cost ratio, utilization, pricing, claims reserves | reserve releases, regulatory changes, timing lags, member mix, working-capital distortion |
| consumer-retail | comparable sales, transactions/ticket, inventory, gross margin, customer mix | inflation vs real volume, calendar shifts, promotions, credit conditions, store openings vs organic demand |

Cross-theme comparisons use evidence breadth/durability and disclosed metric differences, not a pooled ranking of incomparable growth percentages. Geography and customer concentration need explicit treatment. New sectors require their own template before mechanical numerical scoring is attempted.

## Expanded research cohorts

| Theme | Demand and operating measures | Main counterchecks |
|---|---|---|
| banking-credit | net interest income/margin, average loans and deposits, deposit costs/mix, charge-offs, nonperforming loans, provisions and allowance coverage, CET1, fee revenue | deposit repricing vs loan growth, reserve releases vs credit improvement, securities marks, commercial real estate and consumer-credit mix; isolate markets/investment-banking income from lending |
| energy | upstream production and realized prices, unit costs, operating cash flow, sustaining/growth capex, refining margins and downstream volumes | commodity price vs physical volume, acquisitions/divestitures, hedges, working capital and reserve revisions; compare integrated XOM/CVX segments separately from upstream-focused COP/EOG, never pool consolidated margins |
| transport-logistics | freight/carload/package volumes, revenue per unit, fuel-adjusted yield, operating ratio/margin, capacity and service metrics | fuel surcharge vs pricing, shipment mix and calendar effects, labor costs and restructuring; compare UNP/CSX rail and UPS/FDX parcel networks within subgroups before discussing shared demand |
| homebuilding | net orders, cancellations, backlog units/value, closings, selling prices, incentives, gross margin, inventory and land commitments | rate buydowns vs underlying price, seasonality, geographic/product mix, backlog conversion vs new demand, owned vs optioned land and mortgage-finance exposure; preserve each issuer's fiscal period |
| pharmaceuticals | product/franchise revenue and volumes, price/mix, geographic demand, R&D, operating cash flow, disclosed pipeline milestones and patent/exclusivity exposure | rebates/gross-to-net, acquisition and licensing charges, patent erosion, channel inventory, indication/product concentration; distinguish approved commercial revenue from uncertain pipeline value; no probability or regulatory milestone invented from missing inputs |

These are observation cohorts, not claims of homogeneous economics. Establish the latest publicly available issuer quarter first; read comparative original disclosures when needed without requiring a standalone historical report for every period. For industry work, read the matching template and preserve subgroup differences. An undisclosed next quarter is a monitoring condition, not an analysis failure or a substitute period.
